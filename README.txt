# CASIO TOYEN




## Presets

- Piano - analog piano voice from Vermona Piano-Strings
- Klaviphon - electric piano made in Czechoslovakia
- Melodica - cheap Thomann Melodica
- Kalimba - Korg Phase 8 (plucked tone)
- Crystal - Korg Phase 8 (sustained tone)
- Noise - pitched noise
- Groan Tube - groan tube
- Groan Crystal - groan tube with Phase 8 sustained tone
- Taishogoto - single string of Suzuki taishogoto (with some symphatetic resonance from other strings)

## Effect

- Slapack - short delay with lot of feedback
- Lofi - bitcrusher set at 8bit 5500 Hz
- Wow and flutter - delay modulated in a way to simulate unstable pitch of tape player
- Foldback - foldback waveshaper
- Warehouse - full wet sound of very long reverb

# Envelopes

- Gate - instant attack and release, full sustain
- Flute - some attack and release, medium sustain
- Slow flute - slower attack and release, medium sustain
- Ambiance - slow attack and release, less sustain
- Drum - instant attack, medium decay, no sustain
- Burst - only short burst without sustain
